# Community Spotlight Home Page

Welcome to the **Community Spotlight Home Page**, the hub for discovering and documenting the Project!

## About Community Spotlight

Community Spotlight is a **non-profit initiative** designed to offer **free promotional content** to individuals and organizations. Our goal is to enable creativity, collaboration, and an equal chance of visibility for everyone involved.

Take a look at our website! You'll find pages for documentation, promotion forms, and more.
