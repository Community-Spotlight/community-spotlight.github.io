What is Community Spotlight?

Community Spotlight is a non-profit project dedicated to supporting creators, artists, and developers by offering free promotional services.

Our goal is to highlight and showcase talent, projects, and events around the world, providing a platform for visibility and growth. Our Team is constantly working hard to encourage creativity, feature small creators and businesses, and give everyone an equal chance at a platform.

For Advertisers

Community Spotlight allows you to promote your content in the form of promotional Images, Videos, or HTML Embeds. This is all done free of charge!

You have the freedom to promote any work of yours, including games, songs, websites, and more.

Once your Promotion is uploaded, a Team Member will review and add it to our system. Approved promotions become instantly available for any developer using our platform to display and promote.

To upload your Promotions, use our Uploader Form or by clicking the "Submit a Promo" tab above. You'll find our Guidelines and other rules there.

For Developers

Community Spotlight allows you to display Promotions for free with no required Sign-Ups or API keys!

Since we are a non-profit organization, you will not be making any financial profit (This is subject to change). You are simply helping out other people around the world promote their content.

To display Promotions in your project, check out our Developer Exports by clicking the "For Developers" Button above.

Donate

Community Spotlight is free to use and maintained by volunteers. If you'd like to support ongoing development and hosting costs, consider making a donation.
